# M.S. in Computer Science + AI Certificate (USC)

## Summary
I earned my M.S. in Computer Science from USC, awarded August 15, 2025, with a 3.375 GPA over 30 credits in roughly one year. I also earned a Post-Baccalaureate Certificate in Artificial Intelligence concurrently, and completed a published master's thesis in a single summer (~2 months vs. the standard ~2-year timeline).

## Context
I started the M.S. in Fall 2024, right after the B.S. It was a funded position — Graduate Fellow / Graduate Assistant — which mattered a lot: as an international student, USC tuition was prohibitively expensive, and the funded TA position was what made continuing financially viable. I carried 12–13 credits of 700-level graduate coursework per semester while working ~20 hours/week as a TA. (TA role detail is in the experience/usc files.)

## Technical Details
- **Duration:** ~1 year (Fall 2024 – August 2025).
- **Credits:** 30.
- **GPA:** 3.375.
- **Student type:** Graduate Fellow / Graduate Assistant (funded).
- **College:** Molinaroli College of Engineering, USC Columbia.
- **Thesis advisor:** Professor Homayoun Valafar.

The coursework was deliberately AI-heavy: Machine Learning Systems, Neural Networks and Their Applications, Deep Reinforcement Learning, Edge and Neuromorphic Computing, and a Directed Study and Research. (Full course list with grades is in the coursework file.)

## Post-Baccalaureate Certificate in Artificial Intelligence
Earned concurrently with the M.S. by completing ~15 credits of AI-focused graduate coursework. It wasn't a separate program — it fell out naturally from how I selected courses. Contributing courses: Machine Learning Systems, Neural Networks and Their Applications, Deep Reinforcement Learning, Edge and Neuromorphic Computing, Directed Study and Research.

## The Thesis
**Title:** "Multi-Task Deep Learning Approach for Segmenting and Classifying Competitive Swimming Activities Using a Single IMU." Advisor: Professor Homayoun Valafar. Completed in Summer 2025 in ~2 months — the standard M.S. thesis timeline at USC is around 2 years. Published open access on USC Scholar Commons and cited on Google Scholar. (Deep technical detail lives in the research/thesis files; the academic_projects file has the education-context summary.)

## Challenges
Two big ones: the M.S. had to be financially self-funding through the TA role (no TA, no degree), and the thesis was compressed into a single summer while I was wrapping up coursework. Both were delivered.

## Results
M.S. in Computer Science (3.375), AI certificate, and a published, committee-approved thesis completed in ~2 months — all on a funded position that made the degree possible in the first place.

## Lessons Learned
I can operate at high output under extreme time compression — a 2-month thesis against a 2-year norm is the proof. And constraints (funding, timeline) shaped the degree as much as the curriculum did.
