# Education — Overview

## Summary
I earned a B.S. and an M.S. in Computer Science from the University of South Carolina, plus a Post-Baccalaureate Certificate in Artificial Intelligence earned concurrently with the M.S. I did all of it while being a Division I SEC swimmer (for the B.S.) and a funded graduate fellow / TA (for the M.S.).

## Context
I'm from Israel and served 3 years in the Israeli Air Force before university, so I started my B.S. at age 21 — older and more mature than typical freshmen. For the entire B.S. I was also a Division I student-athlete. That dual-load context is the backdrop for everything in the education section: the degrees were earned under significant competing demands, not in a vacuum.

## Institution

## Background Context
- **Military service:** Israeli Air Force, 3 years (mandatory). Started university at 21.
- **Student-athlete:** competitive swimmer for 20 years; swam for USC for 5 seasons (2019–2024) as a Division I SEC athlete. Scored points at SEC Championships (B-final), individually and on relays. (Full detail in the athletics section.)
- **SEC context:** the SEC is the deepest swimming conference in the country — its B/C finals exceed A-finals at most other conferences.

## Timelines
**B.S.:** started Spring 2020 (age 21, post-military), ended May 2024, ~4 years, 132 credits. Multiple Dean's List semesters (Spring 2020, Spring 2022, Fall 2022, Spring 2023, Fall 2023).

**M.S.:** started Fall 2024, ended August 2025, ~1 year, 31 credits. Funded position (Graduate Fellow / Graduate Assistant). Thesis advisor: Professor Homayoun Valafar.

## Results
Two CS degrees plus an AI certificate, both earned while carrying a second full-time commitment (D1 athletics, then a TA role), with multiple Dean's List semesters and a published thesis.

## Lessons Learned
The headline isn't the GPA — it's that the degrees were completed under constant competing load. That's the part that actually predicts how I work.
