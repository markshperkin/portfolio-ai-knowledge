# B.S. in Computer Science (USC)

## Summary
I earned my B.S. in Computer Science from the University of South Carolina, awarded May 4, 2024, with a 3.301 GPA over 132 credits. I did the entire degree as a Division I SEC swimmer, and made the Dean's List in multiple semesters despite that.

## Context
I started the B.S. in Spring 2020 at age 21, after three years in the Israeli Air Force. From day one I was also a full Division I student-athlete — two practices a day, gym, travel for meets — so this degree was never a single-focus undertaking. It was always CS plus 30+ hours a week of athletics.

## Technical Details
- **Duration:** ~4 years (Spring 2020 – May 2024).
- **Credits:** 132.
- **GPA:** 3.301.
- **Dean's List:** Spring 2020, Spring 2022, Fall 2022, Spring 2023, Fall 2023.
- **College:** College of Engineering & Computing, USC Columbia.

The CS curriculum was demanding: heavy math (Calculus I/II, Vector Calculus, Applied Linear Algebra, Discrete Structures, Statistics), low-level systems courses (Digital Logic, Computer Architecture, Operating Systems, UNIX/Linux), theory (Foundations of Computation), and a two-semester senior Capstone. (Full course list with grades is in the coursework file.)

## Challenges
**Calculus II.** I failed Calculus II on the first attempt in Fall 2021 — a hard course made harder by the student-athlete schedule. I retook it in Spring 2022 and earned an A. USC's grade forgiveness policy applied, so only the second grade counts toward GPA. I treat this as a clean example of identifying a weakness and fixing it rather than excusing it.

**The compounding load.** Absorbing rigorous CS and math content while physically exhausted from twice-daily practices, with every-other-weekend meets and a full week away for SEC Championships, meant there was no slack anywhere in the schedule.

## Solutions
Ruthless time management — every gap between practice and class was used. The military discipline carried directly into managing the academic load, and the Calculus II retake showed the recovery pattern: diagnose, redo, beat it.

## Results
B.S. in Computer Science, 3.301 GPA, 132 credits, multiple Dean's List semesters, all completed while scoring at SEC Championships every season of the degree.

## Lessons Learned
A failure on the transcript isn't the story — what you do next is. Calculus II went from an F to an A. That's the pattern I'd want anyone to take from this degree.
