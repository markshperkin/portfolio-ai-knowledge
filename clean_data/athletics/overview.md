# Competitive Swimming — Overview

## Summary
I swam competitively for 20 years, from age 6 to 26. I went from a kid who picked swimming over judo, to an Israeli national champion at 16, to a club-level relay record holder, to a Division I SEC athlete at the University of South Carolina — all while earning a CS degree and serving in the Israeli Air Force. In 2024 I was 35 hundredths of a second from a realistic shot at the Paris Olympics relay. I chose the M.S. in AI instead, and I have zero regrets.

## Context
Swimming is the single biggest thread running through my life before engineering. It's where I learned how I work: long horizons, daily discipline, performing under pressure, and owning outcomes. When I talk about my "athlete's mentality" in a professional setting, this is the 20 years it comes from. It's also directly tied to my graduate research — my master's thesis is a deep learning system for classifying competitive swimming activity from a single IMU, a problem I was uniquely positioned to understand from both sides.

## Technical Details
- **Primary events:** 50m freestyle, 100m freestyle, 100m butterfly. Signature events were the 50 and 100 free.
- **Career span:** age 6–26 (started 2004-ish, retired 2024).
- **Highest level reached:** Division I, SEC (Southeastern Conference) — the deepest swimming conference in the United States.
- **Personal best:** 49.85 in the 100m freestyle long course (2023).

## Timeline (high level)
- Age 6 — started swimming (parents enrolled me alongside judo; I chose swimming).
- Age 11 — qualified for Israeli Nationals for the first time (16th place; top 16 qualify).
- Age 15 (2013) — broke my hand 3 months before nationals, could only kick for 2 months, still won my first medal (2nd).
- Age 16 — Israeli national champion in my age group.
- 2015 — European Youth Olympics, Baku, representing Israel.
- 2016–2019 — Israeli Air Force; earned Elite Athlete status.
- 2017 — Israeli club-level national relay record (Netanya), stood ~2 years.
- 2019 — enrolled at USC at age 21.
- 2019–2024 — 5 seasons as a Division I SEC swimmer at USC.
- 2023 — personal best 49.85 (100m free LC), best shape of my life.
- 2024 — graduated B.S., retired from competitive swimming at 26.
- Today — I swim 1–2x/week, 20–30 min, for health and enjoyment.

## Results
20 years, an Israeli national title, an international meet for my country, a club relay record, 5 seasons scoring at the deepest college conference in the country, and a near-miss on an Olympic relay. More importantly, it built the working habits I bring to engineering.

## Lessons Learned
The biggest takeaway isn't a result — it's the "zero regrets" philosophy: put 100% into everything so that whatever the outcome, you never second-guess whether you did your best. That's how I approach engineering work and career decisions now.
