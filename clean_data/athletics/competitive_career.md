# Competitive Swimming — Career & Achievements

## Summary
My competitive career had two big chapters: the Israeli years (age 6 through my late teens, including the national title and international representation) and the USC years (5 seasons of Division I SEC swimming, 2019–2024). Both chapters involved scoring at the highest level available to me.

## Context
I started swimming at 6 — my parents enrolled me alongside judo and I chose swimming. From age 6 to 10 I swam competitive club swimming in Israel and placed 17th at nationals every year, just short of qualifying. At 11 I finally qualified for Israeli Nationals (16th place — top 16 qualify), and I qualified every year after that.

## Israeli Career Highlights
- Qualified for Israeli Nationals every year from age 11 onward.
- **Israeli National Champion**, 16-and-under age group — the best swimmer in my age group nationally that year.
- Multiple **2nd-place finishes in the 50m freestyle in the senior (open) division** — open to all ages, not just my age group.
- **Israeli club-level national relay record** with Netanya Club in 2017; the record stood for about 2 years.
- Competed in multiple **international meets representing Israel**.
- **European Youth Olympics, Baku, 2015** — representing Israel. It was a huge event: enormous pool, athletes from all over Europe. I collected around 30 different country pins and I still have them.

## USC / College Career Highlights
- **5 seasons of Division I SEC competition** (2019–2024).
- **Scored points at SEC Championships every single season** — both individually (B-final) and on relays.
- **SEC context:** the SEC is widely regarded as the deepest swimming conference in the country. The B-final and C-final competition levels in the SEC exceed the A-finals at most other conferences. Scoring at SEC, even in a B-final, means competing against some of the fastest college swimmers in the United States.
- **Personal best (2023):** 49.85 in the 100m freestyle long course — the fastest I ever swam, set in the best physical shape of my career.

## Events
Primary events: 50m freestyle, 100m freestyle, 100m butterfly. My signature races were the 50 and 100 freestyle — pure sprint events.

## Results
An Israeli national age-group title, repeated senior-division medals in the 50 free, a club relay national record, international representation for Israel, and 5 straight seasons putting points on the board at the deepest conference in US college swimming.

## Lessons Learned
"Always 17th" turning into "qualified at 16th" taught me that the gap between not-quite and good-enough is closed by consistency, not by a single breakthrough. That pattern repeated my whole career.
