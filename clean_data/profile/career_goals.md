# Career Goals

## Summary
I'm targeting AI Engineer, Algorithm Developer/Engineer, and Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) roles. The common thread is production AI systems with sound architecture, built to last — ideally close to the client. I want to be the person who brings AI into industries that don't have it yet.

## Target Roles
**Primary targets:**
- AI Engineer
- Algorithm Developer / Algorithm Engineer
- Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)

**Why FDE specifically:** My RGIS experience taught me this directly. I wasn't in the stakeholder meetings, so I received requirements second-hand. The result was solutions that were technically correct but not precisely tailored to what the client actually needed. FDE permanently solves that — it puts the engineer directly at the client interface. The appeal is the combination of AI/engineering depth with client proximity and product breadth. I want to own the full product lifecycle *and* understand exactly what the client needs, from the source, not filtered.

## Target Company
- **Size:** Preference for enterprise, for large-scale impact — but genuinely open to mid-size and startups where individual impact is just as high.
- **Key requirement:** an environment with experienced engineers to learn from and sharpen my craft against.
- **Industry:** software companies integrating AI into non-AI industries. Most businesses haven't adopted AI yet. There's massive value in being the person who bridges that gap, and that's where I want to be.

## Geography
Currently based in Israel.
- Open to in-person roles in Israel.
- Open to remote (US or otherwise).
- Open to relocating back to the United States.

Preference leans in-person. RGIS was fully remote and the autonomy was valuable, but in-person interaction has real advantages, especially for client-facing and collaborative work.

## Technical Interests
Energized by:
- Agentic AI pipelines — end-to-end design and implementation.
- ML model training and experimentation.
- Scalable system architecture — systems that hold up under pressure and scale without constraint.
- Full product lifecycle ownership.

Not a narrow specialty — the sweet spot is where these intersect: production AI systems with sound architecture built to last.

## What I Avoid
- **Regular weekend work.** Weekends are protected time for family (my wife). Weekday overtime is fine; weekends are a firm boundary.
- **Indirect client communication.** Learned at RGIS that second-hand requirements produce worse outcomes. I want direct access to stakeholders.

## 5-Year Vision
Two paths, both serious:
- **Path 1:** Lead a software engineering team — building and delivering real, measurable value for a company of any size. Technical leadership grounded in actually shipping.
- **Path 2:** Found a software company built on the FDE model — a firm that builds AI solutions tailored precisely to client needs, where engineering and client engagement are inseparable. I have concrete business ideas in development (not public).
