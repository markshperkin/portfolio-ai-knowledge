# Interests and Hobbies

## Summary
Outside of engineering and swimming, my life is: cooking traditional Bukharian food (go-to dish is plov), competitive gaming, traveling whenever I can, going to music concerts (Israeli music + electronic), and freediving in the Red Sea. That's the honest list. I'm not a "no life outside work" person — I have a wife, a culture, and things I actually enjoy.

## Context
The rest of this corpus can read like I spend 100% of my time on AI engineering and competitive swimming. That's not the full picture. This file is the everything-else.

## Cooking
I cook occasionally — not a daily habit, but I love doing it. My **go-to dish is plov** — the traditional Bukharian rice-and-meat dish. Bukharian cuisine is one of the best out there (genuinely), and cooking traditional food is a direct line back to the culture I grew up in.

## Gaming
- **Competitive FPS** is the main thing. Not limited to FPS, but that's the default.
- **Campaign games** I enjoy too — **Half Life** is a favorite.
- I lean competitive over casual.

## Music
- **Israeli music** generally — broad love of the local scene.
- **Electronic** — Techno, House, Deep House.
- **Artists I listen to:** Ravid Plotnik (aka Nechi-Nech), Hadag Nahash.
- I go to **concerts** when I can — live music is a real thing for me, not just background noise.

## Freediving and Scuba
This is the one most people don't expect. I dive — both scuba and freediving.

**Freediving** = diving with **no oxygen**, just one breath. I love it. I've freedived **20+ meters down in Eilat** (the Red Sea, south Israel). It's a totally different headspace from regular scuba — there's a stillness to it that I find addictive.

## What This Tells You About Me
- I'm not single-track. I have culture, relationships, and physical hobbies outside the pool and the laptop.
- I lean toward things that require some level of commitment — competitive games, freediving, traveling somewhere new — over passive consumption.
- The Bukharian/Israeli roots show up everywhere: in the food, the music, the diving spots, the cooking.
