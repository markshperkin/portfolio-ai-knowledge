# RGIS — Results

## Summary
In 3 months as sole developer I took RGIS's lead intelligence from 0% to 50–60% actionable leads, cut pipeline runtime by ~40%, sustained twice-weekly production releases through an international relocation during an active war, and refreshed the company's central AI tool.

## Lead Quality
- **Before:** 0% actionable leads — no prior system existed.
- **After:** 50–60% actionable leads (stakeholder-reported).
- Achieved within 3–4 weeks of starting development.
- Measurement: qualitative stakeholder assessment of lead relevance and usability.

## Pipeline Performance
- Runtime reduced from 20–25 minutes → ~15 minutes through API and LLM optimizations.
- ~40–60 qualified leads per run (20 companies × 2–3 validated C-suite contacts).

## Output Quality
- LLM hallucinations reduced to unnoticeable levels via prompt constraints and increased verified-data usage.
- Each output includes a ranked company list, validated stakeholder contacts, and AI-generated rationale and relevance score per company.

## Delivery
- Twice-weekly production releases maintained for the full 3-month engagement.
- All releases shipped on schedule despite extreme personal circumstances (international relocation, wartime disruption).

## AI-HUB
- Refreshed the visual design of the company's central AI tool.
- Integrated the AI Performance Review service, expanding the HUB's capability footprint for RGIS personnel.

## Personal Growth
- Learned AI-assisted coding tools (OpenCode, Claude Code) in a production context.
- Developed real skill in translating ambiguous, stakeholder-facing requirements into actionable engineering tasks.
- Built a scalable agentic system architecture from scratch as sole developer.
- Learned to work under simultaneous pressure from stakeholders, management, and major personal-life disruption.
- Key lesson: direct stakeholder communication is critical for tailored AI solutions — second-hand requirements introduce a costly ambiguity tax.

## Lessons Learned
The numbers (0→50–60%, ~40% faster) matter, but the durable takeaway is the working model: build for scalability up front, ground the LLM in verified data, and get as close to the stakeholder as possible. The last point reshaped my career targeting toward FDE roles.
